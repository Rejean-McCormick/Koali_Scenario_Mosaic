SCN-002 · Find & Understand

Track competing hypotheses and the evidence that separates them

Koali makes it possible to track competing hypotheses, the evidence that strengthens or weakens them, and the people who hold fragments of the context. The continuity goal is to move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

ExampleA company loses 30% of its sales while customers say they like it more
