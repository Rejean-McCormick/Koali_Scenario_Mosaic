SCN-097 · Remember & Improve

Preserve the history of interventions and their hypotheses

A restaurant changes layout, menu, training, staffing, and reservations together, then credits the last visible change for success. Koali places every adjustment, intended effect, and metric on one timeline, helping the team learn from patterns without claiming causality the evidence cannot support.

ExampleFifty Restaurant Changes. Which Ones Actually Worked?
