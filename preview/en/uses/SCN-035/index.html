SCN-035 · Collaborate & Create

Keep every clause connected to the needs, arguments, objections, and versions that shaped it

A constitution’s final clause can survive while every need, objection, and compromise behind it disappears. Koali preserves the reasoning through branches, merges, and votes, so future citizens can understand the mandate and reopen it without starting from zero.
