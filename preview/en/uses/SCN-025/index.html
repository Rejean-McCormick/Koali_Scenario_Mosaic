SCN-025 · Learn & Share

Keep learning, collaboration, and logistics in the same context

Remote rehearsals, teaching materials, team roles, and a live trip can drift into four incompatible plans. Koali carries one logistical change to every affected person, task, and learning artifact, so preparation and performance evolve together instead of colliding on arrival.
