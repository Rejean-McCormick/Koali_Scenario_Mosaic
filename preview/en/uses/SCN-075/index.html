SCN-075 · Organize & Act

Maintain a common plan where operations and maintenance see the same constraints

Dock repairs fit into tiny windows between tides, inspections, and commercial arrivals that change without warning. Koali gives maintenance and port operations the same live constraints, letting them seize the next safe window without rebuilding context at every shift.
