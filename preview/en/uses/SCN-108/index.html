SCN-108 · Disseminate & Connect to the Public

Separate facts, traditions, and preferences in living culinary knowledge

Koali becomes a global culinary laboratory where scientific fact, tradition, and preference remain distinct; it can turn discoveries into recipes, courses, or media connected to the experiments. The continuity goal is to render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.

ExampleA man maps every ingredient he can find into a living universe of cooking
