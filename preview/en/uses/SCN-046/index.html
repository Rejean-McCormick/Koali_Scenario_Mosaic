SCN-046 · Choose & Govern

Preserve criteria, evidence, authority, and the decision rule

Koali preserves criteria, evidence, and decision authority, records the rule used to decide, then connects the selected supplier to commitments and follow-up that will later show whether the choice remained defensible. The continuity goal is to preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

ExampleA hospital must choose the sole supplier of a vital component
