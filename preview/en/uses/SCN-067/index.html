SCN-067 · Organize & Act

Manage the dependencies required to restore service

Twelve hours of volcanic ash invalidate flights, crews, gates, bags, hotels, and connections at once. Koali holds one recovery picture and ripples every decision through it, so teams restore service together instead of spawning incompatible spreadsheets.
