SCN-084 · Respond & Coordinate

Separate facts, uncertainties, and decisions

Koali separates facts, uncertainties, and decisions; it tracks actions and dependencies without turning the system into a technical guide for manipulating infrastructure. The continuity goal is to move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

ExampleA possible leak appears near a protected watershed
