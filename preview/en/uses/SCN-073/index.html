SCN-073 · Organize & Act

Connect the public mandate to tasks and evidence of execution

A city can deliver an expressway-to-park project on time while quietly abandoning why citizens approved it. Koali turns the reasons for approval into checkpoints the project must satisfy, revealing drift before an on-time delivery replaces its civic purpose.
