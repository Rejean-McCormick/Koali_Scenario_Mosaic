SCN-120 · Disseminate & Connect to the Public

Maintain dependencies between facts and media

In Koali, the same context moves across specialized capabilities without being recreated, from “source knowledge” through “republication”. Without that shared thread, once published, content often becomes detached from its sources, versions, and the people able to update it.

ExampleAn old video becomes suspect as soon as its source knowledge changes
