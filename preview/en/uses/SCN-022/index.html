SCN-022 · Learn & Share

Keep goals, exercises, and observations connected to the same concepts across languages

Therapy fragments when instructions cross languages, move into the home, and sometimes go offline. Koali keeps each local adaptation inside the same care journey, so the family can continue safely and the therapist can see what actually helped.

ExampleRehab Continues When Therapist and Child Share No Language
