SCN-078 · Respond & Coordinate

Link contradictory reports to shared entities and hypotheses

Calls about smoke, noise, and outages may describe one event—or three unrelated emergencies. Koali groups clues around places and entities while keeping rival explanations alive, helping dispatch act on the best current picture without turning an early guess into truth.
