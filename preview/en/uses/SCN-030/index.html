SCN-030 · Learn & Share

Structure long-term expertise into learning tied to its sources

Published papers omit a researcher’s failed paths, debates, methods, and still-open questions. Koali opens those hidden paths as a navigable public curriculum where every lesson can still be traced back to the research that earned it.
