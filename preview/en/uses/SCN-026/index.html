SCN-026 · Learn & Share

Distinguish a recipe from its variants and connect deviations to outcomes

Koali distinguishes the recipe from its variants and connects deviations to outcomes; teams learn why to adjust, not just what to execute. The continuity goal is to capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

ExampleA perfect recipe stops working as soon as the chef is gone
