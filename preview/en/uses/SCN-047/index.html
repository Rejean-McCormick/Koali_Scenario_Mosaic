SCN-047 · Choose & Govern

Preserve baseline support and add an expertise-aware reading

A safe grant proposal can outrank the risky idea that might transform an entire field. Koali separates broad support, expert insight, uncertainty, and minority judgment, then uses future outcomes to test whether the selection process—not only the winner—was sound.
