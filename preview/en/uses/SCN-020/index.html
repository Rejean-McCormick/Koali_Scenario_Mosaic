SCN-020 · Learn & Share

Preserve what was actually known at each moment

Koali preserves the state of knowledge at every moment; Koali can produce exercises and media that remain connected to the incident's real sources. The continuity goal is to capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

ExampleA real cyberattack becomes next year's training simulator
