SCN-070 · Organize & Act

Require preconditions and evidence before each step

In Koali, the same context moves across specialized capabilities without being recreated, from “risk” through “closure”. Without that shared thread, work often arrives without the why, the right context, its dependencies, or the expected proof of result.

ExampleA data center must replace a critical part without interrupting visible service
