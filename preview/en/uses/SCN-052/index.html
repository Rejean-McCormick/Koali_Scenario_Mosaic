SCN-052 · Choose & Govern

Document representation, authority rules, and open objections

In Koali, the same context moves across specialized capabilities without being recreated, from “legitimacy crisis” through “transition”. Without that shared thread, evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.
