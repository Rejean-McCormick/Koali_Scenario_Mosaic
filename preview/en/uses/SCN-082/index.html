Maintain a shared state while multiple centers replan

Weather, an IT failure, and a crew shortage make every airline operations center solve a different crisis. Koali maintains one evolving picture and ripples each flight decision through aircraft, people, gates, and connections, stopping local optimization from breaking the whole network.
