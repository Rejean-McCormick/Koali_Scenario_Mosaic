SCN-049 · Choose & Govern

Connect arguments to observable criteria and follow the decision as a testable hypothesis

A school reform can become doctrine before anyone sees how it affects individual students. Koali attaches every promise to observable outcomes and follows the program as a live experiment, making it possible to learn, correct course, or stop.
