SCN-036 · Collaborate & Create

Connect creative choices, technical constraints, and responsibilities

During a single take, one lighting change can disrupt stunts, sound, camera, and acting within seconds. Koali shows the ripple, reason, timing, and owner of every adjustment, letting six departments adapt as one production rather than six command chains.
