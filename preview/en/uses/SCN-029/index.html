SCN-029 · Learn & Share

Connect learning, real work, and situated evidence so capability can evolve over time

Koali connects learning, real work, and situated evidence so the capability shown can change over time; a title or diploma is no longer the only available proxy for what someone can actually do in a particular domain.

ExampleA school replaces the fixed diploma with a living map of what each person can still do
