SCN-115 · Disseminate & Connect to the Public

Preserve provenance, versions, and links even offline

In Koali, the same context moves across specialized capabilities without being recreated, from “corpus” through “new edition”. Without that shared thread, once published, content often becomes detached from its sources, versions, and the people able to update it.
