# SCN-037 — A robotics team develops a prototype

> **Universal pattern —** Connect exploration, design, testing, decisions, and the next operational step.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-09` Move an idea to a prototype

## Situation

A robotics team develops a prototype. An idea must move through exploration, design, testing, and execution without losing the reasons behind key choices.

## What is usually lost

Ideas, research, trade-offs, and deliverables become separated across tools and teams.

## With Koali

Kristal keeps the relevant knowledge objects, sources, history, relationships, versions, and evidence connected. Konnaxion lets the relevant people discover one another, contribute context, learn, discuss, or co-create around those same objects. Orgo turns the resulting need, decision, or intervention into routed work with responsibilities, dependencies, evidence, and closure.
