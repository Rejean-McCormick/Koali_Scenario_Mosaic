# SCN-053 — A community chooses among several local projects

> **Universal pattern —** Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-11` Deliberate across perspectives

## Situation

A community chooses among several local projects. Different perspectives, interests, and forms of expertise must be visible before a legitimate collective choice is made.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Kristal keeps the relevant knowledge objects, sources, history, relationships, versions, and evidence connected. Konnaxion lets the relevant people discover one another, contribute context, learn, discuss, or co-create around those same objects. Orgo turns the resulting need, decision, or intervention into routed work with responsibilities, dependencies, evidence, and closure. UCKK can turn validated knowledge or experience into learning and multimedia dissemination for the appropriate audience.

## Loop

`projects → contributions → assessments → deliberation → decision → implementation`

## Usage palette

collaborate · deliberate · choose · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **projects → … → implementation** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

enterprise · research · finance

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.**
