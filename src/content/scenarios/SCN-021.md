# SCN-021 — A tour guide must tell the same place to different audiences

> **Universal pattern —** Preserve meaning and learning continuity when language, network access, or location changes.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-06` Teach across language or connectivity constraints

## Situation

A tour guide must tell the same place to different audiences. The learning or knowledge flow must remain usable when language, location, or connectivity changes.

## What is usually lost

Knowledge stays attached to a person, document, or session and does not transfer well over time.

## With Koali

Kristal keeps the relevant knowledge objects, sources, history, relationships, versions, and evidence connected. Konnaxion lets the relevant people discover one another, contribute context, learn, discuss, or co-create around those same objects. UCKK can turn validated knowledge or experience into learning and multimedia dissemination for the appropriate audience.

## Loop

`knowledge → audience → language → narrative → feedback`

## Usage palette

understand · teach-share · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●○○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **UCKK ●●●●○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **knowledge → … → feedback** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

education · technical-trades · health

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Preserve meaning and learning continuity when language, network access, or location changes.**
