# SCN-013 — A family sells its century-old company and everyone wants to protect something different

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

Some want to maximize the price; others want to preserve jobs, the name, the land, or family control. The adviser must understand the real objectives before producing a financially optimal recommendation that could still be humanly disastrous.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Koali connects objectives, rules, assumptions, and consequences for each scenario so the recommendation is traceable rather than a black box.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`assets → people → objectives → constraints → scenarios → trade-offs → recommendation`

understand · collaborate · choose · remember
