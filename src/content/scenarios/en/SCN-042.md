# SCN-042 — One Invisible Contaminant Could Erase Years of Research

> **Universal pattern —** Connect exploration, design, testing, decisions, and the next operational step.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-09` Move an idea to a prototype

## Situation

The protocol must be understood and executed the same way by several teams and instruments. Design decisions are tested, criticized, and revised before launch.

## What is usually lost

Tiny protocol differences become invisible until they destroy comparability across teams and instruments.

## With Koali

Koali turns each rationale into traceable execution and exposes deviations where they happen, stopping an invisible contaminant before it becomes an institutional blind spot.

## Loop

`question → risks → protocol → critiques → simulation → revision → execution`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · create · verify · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **question → … → execution** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to
