# SCN-068 — A scientific campaign heads under the ice with a single four-day weather window

> **Universal pattern —** Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-15` Carry a plan through execution

## Situation

Sampling, vessel, instruments, teams, and permits must align before departure. A missed activity may not be possible again until the following year.

## What is usually lost

Work often arrives without the why, the right context, its dependencies, or the expected proof of result.

## With Koali

Orgo carries the plan through execution; Kristal connects every sample to its context, protocol, and decisions made in the field.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`objectives → protocols → resources → dependencies → window → fieldwork → data → closure`

## Usage palette

understand · organize · act · remember
