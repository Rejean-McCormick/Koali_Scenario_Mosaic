# SCN-071 — No Location, No Lead Actor—and Cameras Roll Today

> **Universal pattern —** Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-15` Carry a plan through execution

## Situation

Hundreds of creative and logistical decisions suddenly become invalid. The team must rebuild the plan without forgetting what can be saved and why certain scenes were designed the way they were.

## What is usually lost

The replacement scene breaks elsewhere because script intent, resources, and dependencies are no longer connected.

## With Koali

Koali propagates the rewrite through shots, resources, and teams while showing what can be saved, so urgency does not erase the film’s intent.

## Loop

`disruption → constraints → options → replanning → roles → shoot → memory`

## Usage palette

understand · organize · act · coordinate

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **disruption → … → memory** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
