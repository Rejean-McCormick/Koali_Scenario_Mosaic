# SCN-026 — The Recipe Fails the Minute the Chef Leaves

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

The problem is not the ingredient list, but dozens of micro-decisions based on texture, humidity, temperature, and equipment. The team turns every service into a source of observations so the standard can evolve without becoming rigid.

## What is usually lost

The ingredient list survives; the chef's moment-by-moment decisions do not.

## With Koali

Koali shows which adjustment answered which texture, temperature, or humidity, so a team learns when to deviate and can succeed after the expert leaves.

## Loop

`recipe → conditions → gestures → outcome → comparison → adjustment → living standard`

## Usage palette

learn · collaborate · teach-share · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **recipe → … → living standard** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to
