# SCN-038 — Bring Back an Abandoned Village Without Inventing Its Past

> **Universal pattern —** Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-07` Co-create without losing the why

## Situation

They reconstruct houses, paths, families, place names, and uses from maps, photos, archives, and memories that sometimes contradict one another. The virtual project then becomes a basis for deciding what could actually be restored or reinvented.

## What is usually lost

Memory becomes fake heritage when documented fact, witness account, and new proposal are merged.

## With Koali

Koali labels archive-backed detail, witness account, and present-day invention before anything enters the model, so experts and families can rebuild together without manufacturing a cleaner past.

## Loop

`traces → memory → map → versions → proposals → co-design → revival`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · create · deliberate · choose · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **traces → … → revival** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
