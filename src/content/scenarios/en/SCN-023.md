# SCN-023 — A museum stops being a place where people look and becomes a place where they investigate

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

Every collection object becomes the starting point for a real problem: provenance, materials, history, conservation, controversy. Visitors build explanations and can compare their reasoning with that of experts.

## What is usually lost

Knowledge stays attached to a person, document, or session and does not transfer well over time.

## With Koali

Kristal provides the evidence graph; KonnectED organizes investigations; useful outputs can join the corpus instead of disappearing after the visit.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`object → question → sources → investigation → production → critique → new resource`

## Usage palette

learn · collaborate · teach-share · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **object → … → new resource** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

museums · citizen science · schools

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.**
