# SCN-058 — A founder dies and leaves the company to a constitution rather than to heirs

> **Universal pattern —** Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-12` Connect decision, mandate, and consequences

## Situation

The constitution defines mission, powers, limits, amendment mechanisms, and the rights of affected groups. Years later, the organization must apply those rules to situations the founder never imagined.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Kristal preserves the text, its rationale, and the history of interpretation; Smart Vote and Orgo apply the chosen mechanisms without pretending to guess "what he would have wanted."

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`constitution → roles → interpretations → decisions → precedents → revision → continuity`

## Usage palette

understand · deliberate · choose · organize · verify

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **constitution → … → continuity** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

companies · foundations · communities

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.**
