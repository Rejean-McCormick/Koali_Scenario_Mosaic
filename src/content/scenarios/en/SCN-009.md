# SCN-009 — When the Textbook Says the Result Cannot Exist

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

A laboratory repeatedly obtains a result that conflicts with the accepted explanation. Instead of discarding it as error, the team precisely preserves the conditions, protocol deviations, and competing hypotheses.

## What is usually lost

An impossible result is discarded when protocol deviations and failed replications have no durable home.

## With Koali

Koali gives the anomaly a durable case—protocol deviations, objections, and replications included—so researchers can challenge consensus without surrendering rigor.

## Loop

`unexpected result → replication → parameters → literature → hypotheses → new tests`
