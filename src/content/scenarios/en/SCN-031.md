# SCN-031 — An inventor wants to prove the history of his idea before paying to protect it

> **Universal pattern —** Connect exploration, design, testing, decisions, and the next operational step.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-09` Move an idea to a prototype

## Situation

At 45, he has a concept he wants recognized without immediately entering an expensive and complex patent process. He documents versions, drawings, tests, known sources, and contributors to establish verifiable provenance, while keeping clear that this record alone does not create patent rights.

## What is usually lost

Ideas, research, trade-offs, and deliverables become separated across tools and teams.

## With Koali

Kristal preserves the genealogy of the invention; Konnaxion can bring together the skills needed to test it; Orgo tracks the validation and disclosure steps chosen by the inventor.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`idea → dated record → versions → tests → contributors → chosen disclosure → prototype`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · create · choose · organize · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **idea → … → prototype** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

innovation · research · intellectual property

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Connect exploration, design, testing, decisions, and the next operational step.**
