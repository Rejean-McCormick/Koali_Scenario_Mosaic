# SCN-084 — A Possible Leak Beside the Protected Watershed

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

The first measurements are ambiguous, but waiting for perfect certainty could worsen the consequences. The team must distinguish precaution, hypothesis, and confirmation while coordinating responsibilities.

## What is usually lost

Uncertainty is either ignored or exaggerated when measurements, decisions, and precautionary actions share no traceable chain.

## With Koali

Koali distinguishes readings, unknowns, precautions, and decisions in one chain, allowing early action without presenting suspicion as established fact.

## Loop

`signal → location → environment → hypotheses → precautionary measures → verification → restoration`
