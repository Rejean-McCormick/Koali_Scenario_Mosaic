# SCN-017 — A new employee inherits ten years of decisions in three weeks

> **Universal pattern —** Carry learning through activity, evidence, and demonstrated situated capability.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-05` Connect learning, practice, and evidence

## Situation

After a merger, she must take over a system that nobody fully understands anymore. Instead of receiving a 900-page binder, she learns by navigating the decisions, incidents, people, and evidence that shaped the system.

## What is usually lost

Knowledge stays attached to a person, document, or session and does not transfer well over time.

## With Koali

Koali turns the real history of work into learning material; what she demonstrates she can do becomes visible through her interventions.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`role → context → learning path → practice → questions → evidence → autonomy`

## Usage palette

learn · find · teach-share · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **role → … → autonomy** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

onboarding · engineering · public administration

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Carry learning through activity, evidence, and demonstrated situated capability.**
