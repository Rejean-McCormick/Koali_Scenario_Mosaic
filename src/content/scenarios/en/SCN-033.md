# SCN-033 — Map a Fictional Universe Without Blurring Canon and Interpretation

> **Universal pattern —** Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-07` Co-create without losing the why

## Situation

After the series' author notices his work, the artist formalizes characters, places, objects, events, and relationships as a navigable universe inside KristALL, whose first major layer already comes from Wikidata. The system must distinguish the real world, published work, adaptation, interpretation, and derivative creation, along with their respective rights.

## What is usually lost

Canon, interpretation, fan invention, and real-world reference collapse into one unreliable universe.

## With Koali

Koali layers every claim with its source, status, and rights, allowing the world to expand creatively without contaminating what the original work actually establishes.

## Loop

`source works → entities → relationships → versions → interpretations → creations → navigable reality`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · create · organize · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **source works → … → navigable reality** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

arts · worldbuilding · digital humanities

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.**
