# SCN-087 — A telecom network suffers a regional outage

> **Universal pattern —** Prevent teams from acting on different or outdated versions of reality.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-17` Maintain a changing shared situation

## Situation

A telecom network suffers a regional outage. The situation changes while several teams are acting, creating a risk that each team works from a different version of reality.

## What is usually lost

Reality changes faster than shared context, so teams risk acting on different or outdated information.

## With Koali
