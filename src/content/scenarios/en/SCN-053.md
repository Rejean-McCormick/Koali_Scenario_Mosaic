# SCN-053 — Break the City's Permanent Inner Circle

> **Universal pattern —** Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-11` Deliberate across perspectives

## Situation

For some projects, temporary teams receive a precise mandate based on expertise, representation, and availability. Power must be time-limited and automatically return to the collective or institution when the mandate ends.

## What is usually lost

Temporary authority becomes permanent through inertia when its rule, duration, and return path are not explicit.

## With Koali

Koali makes appointment, rights, duration, and return of authority explicit, so power expires on schedule unless the collective deliberately renews it.

## Loop

`problem → mandate → team → decision → execution → accountability → role dissolution`

## Usage palette

collaborate · deliberate · choose · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **problem → … → role dissolution** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

municipal government · cooperatives · distributed organizations

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.**
