# SCN-080 — When the Grid Starts Doing What the Manual Never Predicted

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

Several weak anomalies appear at the same time. Operators must distinguish a common cause, secondary effects, and misleading sensors while keeping a precise history of decisions.

## What is usually lost

Operators chase the loudest alarm when weak signals, hypotheses, and confirmations lack one timeline.

## With Koali

Koali aligns signals, hypotheses, actions, and confirmations on one timeline, helping operators test a common cause instead of chasing noise.

## Loop

`signals → grid state → hypotheses → decisions → effects → new measurements → stabilization`

respond · understand · coordinate · remember
