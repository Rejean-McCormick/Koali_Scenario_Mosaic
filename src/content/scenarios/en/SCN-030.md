# SCN-030 — Thirty Years of Research Become a Public Micro-University

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

Her papers represent only a fraction of what she has learned: failures, debates, readings, methods, and open questions. She builds a path where the public can trace conclusions back to their sources.

## What is usually lost

Papers preserve conclusions while failed paths, debates, and open questions remain trapped in one career.

## With Koali

Koali opens those hidden paths as a navigable public curriculum where every lesson can still be traced back to the research that earned it.

## Loop

`corpus → concepts → controversies → paths → exercises → outputs → new corpus`

## Usage palette

understand · learn · teach-share · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **corpus → … → new corpus** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

open science · universities · public scholarship

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.**
