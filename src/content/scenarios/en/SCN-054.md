# SCN-054 — A Masterpiece with Twelve Missing Years

> **Universal pattern —** Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-10` Compare and prioritize explicitly

## Situation

The piece would be a major acquisition, but the legal and ethical risk is real. The committee must compare cultural value, provenance, cost, uncertainty, and the possibility of further research.

## What is usually lost

“Buy” or “reject” erases the provenance uncertainty and conditions that could change the answer.

## With Koali

Koali lets the museum decide conditionally—recording uncertainty, required research, and stopping points—rather than flattening a changing case into “buy” or “reject.”

## Loop

`artwork → criteria → provenance → risks → options → decision → conditions`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · deliberate · choose · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **artwork → … → conditions** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

museums · collections · complex procurement

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.**
