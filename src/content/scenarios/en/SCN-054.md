# SCN-054 — A museum considers buying a masterpiece with a twelve-year gap in its provenance

> **Universal pattern —** Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-10` Compare and prioritize explicitly

## Situation

The piece would be a major acquisition, but the legal and ethical risk is real. The committee must compare cultural value, provenance, cost, uncertainty, and the possibility of further research.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Koali connects the artwork assessment to provenance evidence and can preserve a conditional decision instead of a simple "buy/reject."

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`artwork → criteria → provenance → risks → options → decision → conditions`

understand · collaborate · deliberate · choose · remember
