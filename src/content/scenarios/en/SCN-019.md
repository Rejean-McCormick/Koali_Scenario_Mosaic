# SCN-019 — Before the Bush Pilot Retires, Capture What No Manual Knows

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

Decades of decisions made in cold, wind, and uncertainty risk disappearing with him. He reconstructs real cases while separating rule, intuition, local context, and exception.

## What is usually lost

Rules and intuition blur together when rare flight decisions survive only as retirement stories.

## With Koali

Koali turns edge cases into explorable choices with conditions and consequences, so the next pilots inherit usable judgment rather than folklore.

## Loop

`lived case → conditions → decision → outcome → principle → limits → teaching`

## Usage palette

understand · learn · teach-share · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **lived case → … → teaching** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

aviation · medicine · field operations

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.**
