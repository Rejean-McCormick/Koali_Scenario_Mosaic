# SCN-100 — Document the Restoration So Someone Can Undo It in 50 Years

> **Universal pattern —** Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-21` Preserve capability beyond individuals

## Situation

Every added material, touched area, and reversibility choice must be traceable. The record is not merely a final report, but a transformation map of the object.

## What is usually lost

A future conservator cannot reverse the work if every material and touched area is reduced to a final report.

## With Koali

Koali creates a transformation map of every material, touched area, and choice, letting a future conservator separate original from addition and safely undo the work.

## Loop

`initial state → choices → materials → intervention → evidence → final state → reversibility`

## Usage palette

remember · understand · verify · teach-share

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **initial state → … → reversibility** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.**
