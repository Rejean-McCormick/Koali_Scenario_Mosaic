# SCN-012 — A telescope sees a signal unlike anything in its catalog

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

Before announcing a discovery, the team must rule out instrumentation, noise, known phenomena, and processing error. Distant specialists can propose and test different explanations around the same research object.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Koali preserves the complete path from raw signal through transformations, hypotheses, and critiques so a conclusion remains reconstructable.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`signal → controls → known cases → expertise → hypotheses → replications → interpretation`

find · understand · collaborate · create · remember
