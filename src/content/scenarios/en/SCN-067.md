# SCN-067 — Twelve Hours of Ash: Rebuild the Flight Plan

> **Universal pattern —** Maintain a shared work state across people, teams, resources, and constraints.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-14` Coordinate roles and dependencies

## Situation

Flights, crews, gates, baggage, hotels, connections, and passengers must be reorganized in a system where every decision changes dozens of others.

## What is usually lost

Recovery plans diverge as flights, crews, gates, bags, hotels, and connections update in separate sheets.

## With Koali

Koali holds one recovery picture and ripples every decision through it, so teams restore service together instead of spawning incompatible spreadsheets.

## Loop

`disruption → shared situation → options → reassignments → communications → recovery`

## Usage palette

understand · organize · act · coordinate · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **disruption → … → recovery** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

airports · transportation · complex operations

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Maintain a shared work state across people, teams, resources, and constraints.**
