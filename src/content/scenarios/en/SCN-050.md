# SCN-050 — A committee chooses between a safe return and three transformative bets

> **Universal pattern —** Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-10` Compare and prioritize explicitly

## Situation

The projects differ in scale, horizon, and type of risk. The committee explicitly compares what it is optimizing instead of hiding the decision behind a single score.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Kristal makes criteria, assumptions, and dependencies inspectable; the final decision remains connected to the outcomes that will later make it possible to judge its quality.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`options → criteria → scenarios → risks → comparison → portfolio → follow-up`

## Usage palette

understand · deliberate · choose · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **options → … → follow-up** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

investment · R&D · strategy

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.**
