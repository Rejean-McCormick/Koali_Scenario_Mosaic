# SCN-083 — A mining team loses contact with an underground work area

> **Universal pattern —** Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-18` Keep working in field/offline mode

## Situation

Teams must continue under authorized procedures, incomplete information, and degraded connectivity. Every local observation matters and must later be able to join the common timeline.

## What is usually lost

Reality changes faster than shared context, so teams risk acting on different or outdated information.

## With Koali

Koali supports offline logs and traceable resynchronization; the organization's safety procedures remain the source of operational authority.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`incident → sectors → roles → local work → observations → reconnection → review`
