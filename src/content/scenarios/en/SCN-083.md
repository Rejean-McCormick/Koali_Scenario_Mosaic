# SCN-083 — Silence Underground: Contact with the Mining Team Is Lost

> **Universal pattern —** Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-18` Keep working in field/offline mode

## Situation

Teams must continue under authorized procedures, incomplete information, and degraded connectivity. Every local observation matters and must later be able to join the common timeline.

## What is usually lost

Safety logs fracture underground when authorized procedure, local action, and later synchronization cannot be reconciled.

## With Koali

Koali records local choices offline and later synchronizes them without losing sequence, preserving procedural authority until communication returns.

## Loop

`incident → sectors → roles → local work → observations → reconnection → review`
