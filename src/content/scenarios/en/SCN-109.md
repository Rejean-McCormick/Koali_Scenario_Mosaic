# SCN-109 — Keep Real History and Fictional Worlds in Separate Realities

> **Universal pattern —** Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-23` Turn knowledge into media or learning

## Situation

KristALL, whose large base layer comes from Wikidata converted into Kristal, can contain physical reality, history, hypotheses, and narrative worlds. Each universe has its own entities, rules, versions, sources, and rights.

## What is usually lost

Fiction leaks into fact when realities, rules, sources, versions, and rights are not typed.

## With Koali

Koali gives every reality its own type, sources, versions, and rights, making them explorable together without letting fiction leak into fact.

## Loop

`real world → work → universe → entities → versions → media → updates`

## Usage palette

remember · teach-share · disseminate · learn

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **real world → … → updates** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

digital humanities · arts · encyclopedias

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.**
