# SCN-007 — Every Radio Broadcast Knocks the Network Offline

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

An intermittent outage escapes conventional tools because it depends on an external condition the network team does not measure. Technicians, radio amateurs, and suppliers must connect their observations before the pattern becomes visible.

## What is usually lost

The radio clue vanishes when human observations are dismissed as anecdotes outside network telemetry.

## With Koali

Koali places outage timestamps and human observations on one timeline, giving an apparent anecdote enough structure to become a testable cause.

## Loop

`outage → timestamps → external context → correlations → experts → test → correction`
