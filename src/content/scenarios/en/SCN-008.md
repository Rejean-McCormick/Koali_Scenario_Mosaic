# SCN-008 — A Hairline Crack, 47 Floors Up

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

The developer, engineer, contractor, subcontractors, and manufacturer each hold different documents. A small visible anomaly must be connected to thousands of design decisions, substitutions, and inspections.

## What is usually lost

Responsibility blurs when drawings, substitutions, inspections, and contracts live in separate archives.

## With Koali

Koali reconstructs the chain from substitution approval to installation and inspection, revealing the decisive choice without blurring who was responsible for what.

## Loop

`anomaly → plans → changes → materials → inspections → hypotheses → verification`

understand · find · verify · act
