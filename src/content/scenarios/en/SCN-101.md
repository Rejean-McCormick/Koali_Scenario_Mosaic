# SCN-101 — New Audit, New Names, Same Old Risks

> **Universal pattern —** Compare cases, seasons, incidents, or measures to detect recurrence and change.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-20` See patterns over time

## Situation

Corrective actions, team changes, and rewording create the impression of progress. Multi-year analysis must connect causes, commitments, and actual results.

## What is usually lost

Renamed risks look resolved when corrective actions are closed before their long-term effects are checked.

## With Koali

Koali follows causes and commitments across audit cycles, exposing cosmetic progress and recurring failure even after teams, labels, and reporting structures change.

## Loop

`findings → actions → owners → results → recurrences → systemic cause`

## Usage palette

remember · organize · verify · act
