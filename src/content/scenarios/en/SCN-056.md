# SCN-056 — Let Employees Vote on Strategy—Without Hiding the Weighting

> **Universal pattern —** Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-12` Connect decision, mandate, and consequences

## Situation

Almost every employee votes. Strategic experts are paid to inform and influence the collective through the quality of their analysis; some people are temporarily or permanently ineligible for certain votes for authorized confidential reasons. The company says it has measured the model as more efficient and profitable, and promotes people who genuinely improve decision quality.

## What is usually lost

A weighted result becomes untrustworthy when the baseline vote and influence rules cannot be inspected separately.

## With Koali

Koali keeps the raw ballot and weighted interpretation side by side, explaining influence rules while protecting confidential exclusions, so participation remains both nuanced and auditable.

## Loop

`question → context → analyses → influence → eligibility → Smart Vote → decision → results → learning`
