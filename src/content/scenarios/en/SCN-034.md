# SCN-034 — A startup recruits its team by starting with the problem, not the job titles

> **Universal pattern —** Identify the required capabilities and form the right collective for a goal.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-08` Assemble a team around a problem

## Situation

Instead of opening four job postings, the founders describe the capabilities that are indispensable and look for people whose strengths complement one another. An unconventional person can become central because evidence of their practice fills exactly the missing gap.

## What is usually lost

Ideas, research, trade-offs, and deliverables become separated across tools and teams.

## With Koali

Konnaxion reveals complementarities from demonstrated skills and contributions; Orgo then turns the emerging team into real responsibilities.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`problem → capabilities → people → complementarities → commitments → team`

## Usage palette

find · collaborate · create · organize · learn

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **problem → … → team** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

entrepreneurship · civic projects · research

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Identify the required capabilities and form the right collective for a goal.**
