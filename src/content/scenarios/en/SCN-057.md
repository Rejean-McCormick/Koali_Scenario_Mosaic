# SCN-057 — Program the Divisive Work—or Protect the Festival?

> **Universal pattern —** Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-12` Connect decision, mandate, and consequences

## Situation

Artistic value, freedom of expression, safety, mission, partners, and local context collide. The committee wants to avoid both moral panic and indifference to consequences.

## What is usually lost

Minutes record the verdict but lose the mission, objections, safety concerns, and responsibility behind it.

## With Koali

Koali keeps objections and accountable owners beside the verdict, so the choice survives scrutiny and can be revisited when conditions change.

## Loop

`work → mission → risks → perspectives → options → mandate → decision → communication`

## Usage palette

collaborate · deliberate · choose · organize · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **work → … → communication** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

culture · universities · media

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.**
