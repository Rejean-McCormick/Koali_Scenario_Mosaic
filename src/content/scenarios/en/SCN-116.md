# SCN-116 — Build a Community Channel with Public, Restricted, and Sacred Doors

> **Universal pattern —** Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-23` Turn knowledge into media or learning

## Situation

The goal is not to "put the culture on the Internet." The community controls who can publish, see, comment on, translate, or reuse each type of knowledge, and preserves the provenance of contributions.

## What is usually lost

Publishing without object-level permissions can expose sacred knowledge or silence the community entirely.

## With Koali

Koali applies permission and provenance to each knowledge object, allowing people to publish, translate, discuss, and reuse only through the doors they choose.

## Loop

`knowledge → rules → roles → publication → access → transmission → governance`

## Usage palette

collaborate · teach-share · disseminate · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **knowledge → … → governance** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

communities · heritage · organizations

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.**
