# SCN-111 — Publish a Disappearing Trade Before the Last Expert Leaves

> **Universal pattern —** Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-23` Turn knowledge into media or learning

## Situation

Tutorials, frequent mistakes, materials, variants, and case stories are gathered with their sources. The public can learn, but the system clearly distinguishes what requires supervision or professional qualification.

## What is usually lost

The “ultimate course” becomes obsolete the moment its cases, cautions, and source practice are severed.

## With Koali

Koali embeds mistakes, cases, cautions, and source practice into the learning, so future craftspeople inherit adaptable judgment instead of a frozen “ultimate course.”

## Loop

`practice → capture → modules → demonstrations → limits → publication → feedback`

## Usage palette

learn · teach-share · disseminate · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **practice → … → feedback** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.**
