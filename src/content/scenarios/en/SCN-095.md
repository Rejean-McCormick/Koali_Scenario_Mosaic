# SCN-095 — The Lab's Most Valuable Archive Is Full of Failures

> **Universal pattern —** Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-21` Preserve capability beyond individuals

## Situation

Negative results, protocol variants, and dead ends are documented as carefully as successes. Other teams avoid months of work thanks to this "graveyard" of paths.

## What is usually lost

Failed paths vanish from the scientific record, forcing other teams to repeat them.

## With Koali

Koali preserves the negative result with protocol, context, and provenance, making failure searchable and reproducibility honest about what did not work.

## Loop

`hypothesis → protocol → result → useful failure → conditions → reuse → new hypothesis`

## Usage palette

remember · understand · teach-share · verify

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **hypothesis → … → new hypothesis** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

research · R&D · innovation

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.**
