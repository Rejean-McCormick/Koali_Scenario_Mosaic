# SCN-079 — Run a Humanitarian Response on a Flickering Satellite Link

> **Universal pattern —** Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-18` Keep working in field/offline mode

## Situation

Field teams must make decisions with imperfect data, share inventory and needs, then synchronize when a communication window opens.

## What is usually lost

The newest satellite update wins by timestamp even when an older local observation is better supported.

## With Koali

Koali lets the field operate from its own coherent picture and merges updates by provenance—not recency—so coordination never overrules better-supported observations.

## Loop

`needs → inventory → local teams → offline work → transfers → synchronization → new needs`

## Usage palette

respond · understand · collaborate · organize · act · coordinate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **needs → … → new needs** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

humanitarian response · field operations · logistics

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.**
