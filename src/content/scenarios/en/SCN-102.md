# SCN-102 — The Bug Hiding Inside 10,000 Customer Complaints

> **Universal pattern —** Connect results, decisions, and causes into usable lessons learned.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-19` Turn action into a lesson

## Situation

Every call seems different, but connected contexts reveal the same need or defect under varied wording. Agents become sensors for product knowledge.

## What is usually lost

Ten thousand unique tickets conceal one defect when wording cannot be connected to shared concepts.

## With Koali

Koali recognizes the shared concept and closes the loop through product decisions and frontline guidance, allowing one fix to resolve a pattern instead of ten thousand tickets.

## Loop

`requests → concepts → clusters → hypotheses → product → change → new requests`

## Usage palette

remember · understand · collaborate · act

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **requests → … → new requests** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

customer service · software · public services

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Connect results, decisions, and causes into usable lessons learned.**
