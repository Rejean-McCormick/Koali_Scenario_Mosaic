# SCN-001 — Forty Minutes Below −35 °C: The Generator Quits

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

In a northern village, the backup generator shuts down only during extreme cold, always after almost the same amount of time. Logs, maintenance habits, replaced parts, and operator observations each tell a different part of the story.

## What is usually lost

A frost-only failure stays mysterious when telemetry, repair history, and operator memory are filed apart.

## With Koali

Koali assembles the whole investigation around the machine, so even a failed test becomes evidence and the rare fault can finally be explained.

## Loop

`rare symptom → history → conditions → comparable cases → hypotheses → test → cause → memory`
