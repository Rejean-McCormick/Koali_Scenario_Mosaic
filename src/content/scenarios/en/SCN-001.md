# SCN-001 — An engine fails only at −35 °C, after exactly forty minutes

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

In a northern village, the backup generator shuts down only during extreme cold, always after almost the same amount of time. Logs, maintenance habits, replaced parts, and operator observations each tell a different part of the story.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Koali connects readings, past interventions, and human observations to the same technical object; every tested hypothesis stays attached to its outcome, even when it proves wrong.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`rare symptom → history → conditions → comparable cases → hypotheses → test → cause → memory`
