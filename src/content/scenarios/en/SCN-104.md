# SCN-104 — Grades Rise—While a Small Group Moves Closer to Dropping Out

> **Universal pattern —** Connect results, decisions, and causes into usable lessons learned.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-19` Turn action into a lesson

## Situation

An aggregate indicator tells a success story while individual trajectories reveal an unexpected consequence. Evaluation must preserve multiple dimensions instead of collapsing them into one score.

## What is usually lost

The average grade hides which students paid for the program's apparent success.

## With Koali

Koali reveals effects group by group against the original aims, so leaders can see who benefited, who paid, and where to adjust.

## Loop

`program → hypotheses → cohorts → results → subgroups → revision → new pilot`
