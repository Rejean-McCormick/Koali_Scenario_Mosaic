# SCN-051 — A team hires for a strategic role

> **Universal pattern —** Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-10` Compare and prioritize explicitly

## Situation

A team hires for a strategic role. Several options compete for limited attention or resources, and the decision needs explicit criteria and evidence.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Kristal keeps the relevant knowledge objects, sources, history, relationships, versions, and evidence connected. Konnaxion lets the relevant people discover one another, contribute context, learn, discuss, or co-create around those same objects. Orgo turns the resulting need, decision, or intervention into routed work with responsibilities, dependencies, evidence, and closure.

## Loop

`need → criteria → evidence → evaluations → decision → onboarding`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · choose · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **need → … → onboarding** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

enterprise · public-governance · research

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.**
