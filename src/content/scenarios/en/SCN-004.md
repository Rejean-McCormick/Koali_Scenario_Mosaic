# SCN-004 — A part breaks only on the night shift, three weeks after it is made

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

A factory produces thousands of compliant parts except for a small batch that always cracks after a similar delay. Possible causes cross the supplier, humidity, material recipe, machine settings, and an informal practice unique to one team.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Koali brings together batches, parameters, changes, suppliers, and field practices so the real cause does not disappear between quality systems.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`defect → batches → conditions → people → hypotheses → tests → root cause → correction`
