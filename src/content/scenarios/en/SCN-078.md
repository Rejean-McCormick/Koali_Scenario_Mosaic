# SCN-078 — Within minutes, 911 receives different calls that may describe the same event

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

Noise, smoke, outages, and contradictory testimony may belong to one incident or several. Signals must be grouped without reaching a conclusion too early.

## What is usually lost

Reality changes faster than shared context, so teams risk acting on different or outdated information.

## With Koali

Kristal connects reports and confidence levels; Orgo coordinates authorized responses and their updates.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`calls → location → correlations → hypotheses → resources → situation → resolution`

## Usage palette

respond · understand · coordinate · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●○○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **calls → … → resolution** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

emergency response · civil protection · operations centers

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.**
