# SCN-061 — The elevator fails in a heat wave, with vulnerable residents upstairs

> **Universal pattern —** Give the responsible person the task, the why, the history, the constraints, and the expected evidence of completion.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-13` Deliver work with its context

## Situation

The technical problem immediately becomes a human one. Repair, access, deliveries, visits, and resident information must move together without every team restarting the investigation.

## What is usually lost

Work often arrives without the why, the right context, its dependencies, or the expected proof of result.

## With Koali

Orgo hands off each task with the context that justifies it; Kristal connects equipment, history, and resident constraints; useful updates automatically reach the people concerned.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`report → building context → affected people → intervention → handoff → proof of restoration`

## Usage palette

understand · organize · act · verify · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●○○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **report → … → proof of restoration** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

buildings · public services · operations

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Give the responsible person the task, the why, the history, the constraints, and the expected evidence of completion.**
