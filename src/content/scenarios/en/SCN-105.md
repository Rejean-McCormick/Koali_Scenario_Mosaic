# SCN-105 — Publish the Forecast Before the Policy Changes Reality

> **Universal pattern —** Compare cases, seasons, incidents, or measures to detect recurrence and change.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-20` See patterns over time

## Situation

Predictions, assumptions, indicators, and timelines are recorded before implementation. Two years later, citizens and decision-makers can compare intention, forecast, and result without retrospective rewriting.

## What is usually lost

A policy cannot learn from its forecast when intention, assumptions, and results are rewritten after the fact.

## With Koali

Koali freezes predictions and assumptions before implementation, then compares them with results so gaps become public learning rather than retrospective storytelling.

## Loop

`policy → hypotheses → predictions → implementation → results → comparison → learning`

## Usage palette

remember · understand · deliberate · choose

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **policy → … → learning** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
