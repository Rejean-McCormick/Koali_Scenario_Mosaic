# SCN-099 — Thirty Seasons of Weather Hidden in One Farmer's Fields

## Situation

He connects dates, varieties, soil, snow, winds, pests, and yields plot by plot. Traditional practices and instrument data can be compared rather than opposed.

## What is usually lost

National averages erase the plot-level links between snow, soil, variety, pests, and yield.

## With Koali

Koali builds plot-level memory across thirty seasons, letting traditional practice and instrument data explain one another and making local patterns reusable.
