# SCN-027 — Two Practice Windows a Year: How Do You Train?

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

The real opportunity is too rare for learning by repetition. Every intervention is therefore captured as a case, including preparation, signs to recognize, decisions, and evidence of success.

## What is usually lost

Two annual practice windows cannot build mastery if each intervention disappears after completion.

## With Koali

Koali preserves preparation, signals, choices, and outcomes as a replayable case, letting learners rehearse between events and prove readiness when the next window opens.

## Loop

`rare intervention → capture → simulation → practice → assessment → next intervention`

## Usage palette

learn · teach-share · organize · act

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **rare intervention → … → next intervention** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

maintenance · public utilities · skilled trades

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.**
