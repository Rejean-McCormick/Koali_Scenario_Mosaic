# SCN-065 — Ready Every Snowplow Before the Historic Storm

> **Universal pattern —** Maintain a shared work state across people, teams, resources, and constraints.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-14` Coordinate roles and dependencies

## Situation

Every vehicle has a different history, parts are missing, and some repairs depend on the same small group of experts. The maintenance campaign becomes a real-time capacity and prioritization problem.

## What is usually lost

A snowplow looks ready on paper while its missing part and unavailable mechanic sit in other systems.

## With Koali

Koali exposes the true blocker across fleet, inventory, and qualified people, then verifies that each plow is operational before the historic storm arrives.

## Loop

`fleet → condition → parts → skills → priorities → interventions → availability`

## Usage palette

understand · organize · act · verify · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●○○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **fleet → … → availability** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Maintain a shared work state across people, teams, resources, and constraints.**
