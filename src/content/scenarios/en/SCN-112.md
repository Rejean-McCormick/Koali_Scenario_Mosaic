# SCN-112 — Climate data becomes a story every village can recognize

> **Universal pattern —** Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-22` Explain publicly without severing sources

## Situation

Abstract curves are connected to frost dates, roads, harvests, species, memories, and locally observed changes. The narrative remains connected to the data so an anecdote does not become general proof.

## What is usually lost

Once published, content often becomes detached from its sources, versions, and the people able to update it.

## With Koali

Kristal connects scientific data and testimonies with distinct statuses; UCKK produces maps, stories, or songs adapted to local context.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`data → places → observations → narrative → media → discussion → new observations`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · teach-share · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **data → … → new observations** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

climate · education · local media

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.**
