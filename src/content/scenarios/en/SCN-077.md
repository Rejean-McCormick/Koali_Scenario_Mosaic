# SCN-077 — A wildfire cuts roads, power, and almost all communications to a small town

> **Universal pattern —** Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-18` Keep working in field/offline mode

## Situation

Isolated teams keep working with offline devices. When a connection returns, their observations and actions must resynchronize without overwriting information collected elsewhere.

## What is usually lost

Reality changes faster than shared context, so teams risk acting on different or outdated information.

## With Koali

Koali supports continuity of disconnected work and later reconciles local logs with provenance and visible conflicts.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`alert → sectors → local plans → offline work → observations → synchronization → common situation`
