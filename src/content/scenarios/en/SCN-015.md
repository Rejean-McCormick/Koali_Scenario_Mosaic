# SCN-015 — Saving a manuscript requires four experts who never work together

> **Universal pattern —** Connect a question, need, or project with the relevant people and knowledge.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-03` Find the right expertise

## Situation

No single expert can solve the problem. The challenge is to identify complementary capabilities, including tacit knowledge held by someone who does not describe themselves as an "expert."

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Konnaxion forms the team from demonstrated capabilities and the precise context of the problem rather than from job titles alone.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`problem → required capabilities → people → evidence of practice → team → intervention`

## Usage palette

find · collaborate · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **problem → … → intervention** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
