# SCN-059 — Kill Nine Prototypes. Keep Every Lesson.

> **Universal pattern —** Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-12` Connect decision, mandate, and consequences

## Situation

Every prototype has champions, sunk costs, and a history. Governance must decide which ones to pursue while preserving knowledge from rejected paths so they can be revived if the context changes.

## What is usually lost

Killing a prototype also kills its evidence when rejection is treated as deletion.

## With Koali

Koali keeps failures, tests, and assumptions available after rejection, so tomorrow’s changed constraint can revive a useful path without forcing the team to rediscover its lessons.

## Loop

`prototypes → criteria → evidence → comparison → stop/continue → memory → reopening`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · choose · organize · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **prototypes → … → reopening** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

R&D · product portfolio · innovation

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.**
