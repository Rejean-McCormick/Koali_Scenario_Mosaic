# SCN-040 — Memory, Trees, Housing, Access: One Square Must Hold Them All

> **Universal pattern —** Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-07` Co-create without losing the why

## Situation

Architects, elders, children, merchants, and people with reduced mobility do not use the place in the same way. Models evolve through conflict instead of hiding it behind a symbolic consultation.

## What is usually lost

The loudest stakeholder wins when everyday uses and design consequences cannot be compared on one map.

## With Koali

Koali maps everyday uses and design consequences together, letting affected people shape the decisive choices before they harden into concrete.

## Loop

`uses → perspectives → constraints → prototypes → tests → trade-offs → place`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · create · deliberate · choose · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **uses → … → place** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

urban planning · architecture · public design

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.**
