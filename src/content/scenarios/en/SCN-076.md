# SCN-076 — A Hostage Crisis Where Not Everyone Can Know Everything

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

Facts change quickly, sources do not all have the same reliability, and some information must remain within a very small circle. The system must support shared situational awareness, the chain of decision, and post-incident learning without detailing or enabling methods of violence.

## What is usually lost

Fast facts leak too widely—or arrive too late—when access, source reliability, and decision history are separated.

## With Koali

Koali lets different authorized circles work from the same incident without seeing everything, preserving decisions and time so teams move quickly, avoid leaks, and learn safely afterward.

## Loop

`report → verified facts → roles → decisions → authorized actions → new facts → resolution → after-action review`

## Usage palette

respond · understand · collaborate · organize · act · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **report → … → after-action review** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

public safety · crisis management · continuity

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.**
