# SCN-003 — Coup Footage—or a Near-Perfect Fake?

> **Universal pattern —** Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-02` Reconstruct context and provenance

## Situation

A newsroom receives videos, eyewitness accounts, official statements, and satellite images that contradict one another. Rather than manufacture a single story too quickly, it must show what is confirmed, probable, disputed, or still unknown.

## What is usually lost

A convincing clip becomes “fact” when claims outrun their sources, versions, and confidence levels.

## With Koali

Koali shows provenance, confidence, and disagreement together, so a newsroom can publish what is known without disguising uncertainty as fact.

## Loop

`sources → provenance → timeline → contradictions → confidence levels → verifiable account`

## Usage palette

find · understand · collaborate · verify

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **sources → … → verifiable account** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

journalism · history · open-source intelligence

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.**
