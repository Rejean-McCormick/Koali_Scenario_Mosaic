# SCN-086 — A Burst Pipe, 20 Floors Up, at −25 °C

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

Plumbing, electricity, elevators, residents, the insurer, and suppliers become interdependent. Context must move from one team to the next with every task.

## What is usually lost

Each handoff restarts the emergency when building systems, damage, residents, insurer, and suppliers lack one state.

## With Koali

Koali carries one live damage picture through every handoff and confirmation, so the next team advances the response instead of restarting it.

## Loop

`alert → zone → people → authorized shutoffs → interventions → drying → restoration`

## Usage palette

respond · organize · act · remember · understand

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●○○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **alert → … → restoration** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to
