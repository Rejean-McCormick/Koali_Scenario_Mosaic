# SCN-002 — A company loses 30% of its sales while customers say they like it more

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

Satisfaction indicators are rising and the product has never been more stable, yet orders are suddenly falling. The team discovers that the signal may not be in the product itself, but in purchasing rules, intermediaries, and an invisible shift in market behavior.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Koali makes it possible to track competing hypotheses, the evidence that strengthens or weakens them, and the people who hold fragments of the context.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`commercial signal → data → external context → hypotheses → experts → tests → explanation`
