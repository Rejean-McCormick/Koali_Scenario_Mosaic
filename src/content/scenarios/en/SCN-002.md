# SCN-002 — Customer Love Is Up. Sales Are Down 30%.

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

Satisfaction indicators are rising and the product has never been more stable, yet orders are suddenly falling. The team discovers that the signal may not be in the product itself, but in purchasing rules, intermediaries, and an invisible shift in market behavior.

## What is usually lost

Sales data, customer sentiment, and purchasing-rule changes point in different directions unless their context is reunited.

## With Koali

Koali turns the contradiction into a shared investigation, helping the team expose the purchasing-rule change and preserve why its next move makes sense.

## Loop

`commercial signal → data → external context → hypotheses → experts → tests → explanation`
