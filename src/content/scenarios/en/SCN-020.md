# SCN-020 — Rebuild the Cyberattack as Tomorrow's Simulator

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

Instead of writing a forgotten post-mortem, the team turns the timeline, missed signals, and decisions into a learning scenario. Future analysts replay the incident with only the information that was available at each moment.

## What is usually lost

A post-mortem becomes fiction when hindsight overwrites what the team actually knew at each moment.

## With Koali

Koali can replay the incident with only the knowledge available at each step, creating exercises that train judgment under uncertainty instead of rewarding answers learned afterward.

## Loop

`incident → timeline → decisions → lessons → simulation → practice → new evidence`

## Usage palette

remember · learn · teach-share · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **incident → … → new evidence** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

cybersecurity · emergency response · operations

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.**
