# SCN-096 — The Person Who Solves the Impossible Retires Friday

> **Universal pattern —** Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-21` Preserve capability beyond individuals

## Situation

His talent rests on dozens of patterns he recognizes without naming them. Before he leaves, the organization reconstructs real cases and the cues that triggered his decisions.

## What is usually lost

Pattern recognition walks out the door when expert cues survive only as undocumented instinct.

## With Koali

Koali turns the expert’s cues and edge judgments into a challengeable playbook, so successors inherit know-how they can test and improve—not just instructions.

## Loop

`case → cues → hypotheses → decision → outcome → principles → transmission`

## Usage palette

remember · teach-share · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **case → … → transmission** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

maintenance · diagnosis · rare expertise

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.**
