# SCN-014 — One street becomes unlivable during heat waves while neighboring streets remain tolerable

> **Universal pattern —** Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-01` Investigate a signal

## Situation

The city overlays temperature, materials, trees, shade, traffic, buildings, and resident testimony. The problem looks local, but its cause may come from a combination of decisions made over several decades.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Koali connects data layers to the history of decisions and citizen observations, turning a hot spot on a map into an actionable explanation.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`heat signal → map → urban history → hypotheses → simulations → interventions → measurement`
