# SCN-085 — Different Arrivals, Same Disease at the Shelter

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

The cases may be connected by transport, environment, food, or coincidence. Teams must map contacts and observations while continuing ordinary care.

## What is usually lost

Coincidence looks like contagion when contacts, arrivals, environments, and observations cannot be compared.

## With Koali

Koali maps contacts, environments, food, and symptoms while ordinary care continues, letting the explanation evolve without stigmatizing people on the first pattern.

## Loop

`cases → contacts → batches → environment → hypotheses → measures → follow-up`

## Usage palette

respond · understand · collaborate · organize · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **cases → … → follow-up** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
