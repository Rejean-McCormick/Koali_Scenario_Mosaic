# SCN-049 — A school decides to eliminate almost all age-based classes

> **Universal pattern —** Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-11` Deliberate across perspectives

## Situation

Students would progress through a skills graph instead, with changing groups based on need and mastery. Parents, teachers, and students must debate equity, socialization, logistics, and evidence of learning.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Koali connects arguments to observable criteria and lets the selected program be followed as a hypothesis to test, not a doctrine.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`proposal → objectives → perspectives → risks → pilots → deliberation → decision`

## Usage palette

understand · collaborate · deliberate · choose · learn

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **proposal → … → decision** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

education · training · institutional policy

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.**
