# SCN-073 — A city turns an expressway into a park without stopping the city

> **Universal pattern —** Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-15` Carry a plan through execution

## Situation

The political decision is only the beginning. Transportation, businesses, emergency services, construction, communications, and temporary phases must translate an abstract intention into reality.

## What is usually lost

Work often arrives without the why, the right context, its dependencies, or the expected proof of result.

## With Koali

Orgo connects the public mandate to tasks and evidence of execution; Kristal makes it possible to verify whether the final project still respects the reasons behind the decision.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`decision → objectives → phases → roles → dependencies → construction → measurements → adjustments`

## Usage palette

choose · organize · act · verify · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **decision → … → adjustments** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

infrastructure · public policy · major projects

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.**
