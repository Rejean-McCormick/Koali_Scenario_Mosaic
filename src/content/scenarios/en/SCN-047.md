# SCN-047 — A science fund can finance five ideas out of fifty, and one could transform an entire field

> **Universal pattern —** Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-10` Compare and prioritize explicitly

## Situation

The safest projects are not necessarily the most important. Reviewers must make their criteria, uncertainty, and preferences visible rather than producing an opaque average.

## What is usually lost

Evidence, arguments, disagreements, and reasons for a choice become detached from the decision and its consequences.

## With Koali

Koali makes it possible to compare heterogeneous options explicitly and later revisit whether the chosen criteria actually predicted value.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`projects → criteria → assessments → uncertainty → portfolio → decision → results`

## Usage palette

understand · deliberate · choose · organize

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **projects → … → results** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
