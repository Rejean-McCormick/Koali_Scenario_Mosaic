# SCN-047 — Five Grants, Fifty Ideas, One Possible Breakthrough

> **Universal pattern —** Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-10` Compare and prioritize explicitly

## Situation

The safest projects are not necessarily the most important. Reviewers must make their criteria, uncertainty, and preferences visible rather than producing an opaque average.

## What is usually lost

A single ranking buries uncertainty, minority judgments, and the reasons a risky idea was rejected.

## With Koali

Koali separates broad support, expert insight, uncertainty, and minority judgment, then uses future outcomes to test whether the selection process—not only the winner—was sound.

## Loop

`projects → criteria → assessments → uncertainty → portfolio → decision → results`

understand · deliberate · choose · organize
