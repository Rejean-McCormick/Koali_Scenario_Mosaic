# SCN-032 — Five Countries, One Disease, No Shared Scientific Language

> **Universal pattern —** Identify the required capabilities and form the right collective for a goal.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-08` Assemble a team around a problem

## Situation

The data is not homogeneous, scientific cultures differ, and several hypotheses compete. The consortium forms around the problem rather than around a dominant institution.

## What is usually lost

Five teams appear aligned while their terms, evidence standards, and confidence levels mean different things.

## With Koali

Koali lets them work on shared cases while definitions, standards, and confidence stay explicit, enabling coordination without a false scientific translation.

## Loop

`problem → capabilities → teams → data → hypotheses → shared protocols → results`

## Usage palette

find · understand · collaborate · create · organize

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **problem → … → results** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

international research · health · climate

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Identify the required capabilities and form the right collective for a goal.**
