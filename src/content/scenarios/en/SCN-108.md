# SCN-108 — A man maps every ingredient he can find into a living universe of cooking

> **Universal pattern —** Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-23` Turn knowledge into media or learning

## Situation

Each ingredient is connected to taste, texture, chemistry, season, origin, transformations, substitutes, allergies, traditions, and experiments. Recipes become structured experiments, and the system begins exploring possible combinations beyond recipes that have already been written.

## What is usually lost

Once published, content often becomes detached from its sources, versions, and the people able to update it.

## With Koali

Kristal becomes a global culinary laboratory where scientific fact, tradition, and preference remain distinct; UCKK can turn discoveries into recipes, courses, or media connected to the experiments.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`ingredients → properties → techniques → combinations → experiments → results → new possibilities`

## Usage palette

teach-share · disseminate · learn · collaborate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **ingredients → … → new possibilities** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

cooking · agriculture · food science
