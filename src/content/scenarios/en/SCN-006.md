# SCN-006 — The Masterpiece with Three Pasts

> **Universal pattern —** Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-02` Reconstruct context and provenance

## Situation

Weeks before a major exhibition, new documents suggest the work changed hands under poorly documented circumstances. The museum must reconstruct provenance without erasing uncertainty or treating an old internal record as final truth.

## What is usually lost

A provenance gap hardens into a false certainty when evidence and competing histories are flattened.

## With Koali

Koali lets curators compare rival histories without erasing any trail; each discovery changes the confidence of the story instead of silently rewriting it.

## Loop

`object → owners → documents → versions → contradictions → expertise → provisional conclusion`

## Usage palette

find · understand · collaborate · verify · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **object → … → provisional conclusion** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
