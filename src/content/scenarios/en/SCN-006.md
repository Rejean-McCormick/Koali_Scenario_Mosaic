# SCN-006 — A museum discovers that its star artwork has three incompatible origin stories

> **Universal pattern —** Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-02` Reconstruct context and provenance

## Situation

Weeks before a major exhibition, new documents suggest the work changed hands under poorly documented circumstances. The museum must reconstruct provenance without erasing uncertainty or treating an old internal record as final truth.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Kristal represents the provenance chain, evidence quality, and competing versions; each new discovery can change the picture without rewriting the earlier history.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`object → owners → documents → versions → contradictions → expertise → provisional conclusion`

find · understand · collaborate · verify · remember
