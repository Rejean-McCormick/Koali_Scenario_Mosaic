# SCN-074 — A laboratory must collect one thousand comparable samples at one hundred different sites

> **Universal pattern —** Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-15` Carry a plan through execution

## Situation

A variation in method, labeling, or temperature can destroy comparability. The plan must coordinate people, equipment, protocols, schedules, and chains of custody.

## What is usually lost

Work often arrives without the why, the right context, its dependencies, or the expected proof of result.

## With Koali

Orgo carries the logistical dependencies; Kristal connects every sample to its origin, conditions, and evidence of compliance.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`protocol → sites → kits → roles → collection → validation → data`

## Usage palette

organize · act · verify · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●○○○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **protocol → … → data** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
