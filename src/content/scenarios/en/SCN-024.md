# SCN-024 — What Does “Smoky” Taste Like to Ten Different People?

> **Universal pattern —** Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-04` Turn practice into transferable knowledge

## Situation

Words such as "smoky," "mineral," or "floral" seem precise until ten people use them differently. The team connects sensory descriptions, samples, provenance, and comparisons to build a living reference system.

## What is usually lost

A vocabulary stays vague when words are never calibrated against shared sensory examples.

## With Koali

Koali anchors vocabulary to shared samples and situations, letting a group calibrate perception until its words become precise enough to teach and compare.

## Loop

`sensory experience → vocabulary → examples → comparison → calibration → practice`

## Usage palette

learn · collaborate · teach-share · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **sensory experience → … → practice** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

food · perfumery · quality control

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.**
