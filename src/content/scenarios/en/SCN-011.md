# SCN-011 — Two sisters discover that the same ancestor has three official identities

> **Universal pattern —** Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-02` Reconstruct context and provenance

## Situation

Civil records, religious registers, annotated photos, and family stories do not agree. The goal is not to choose one version arbitrarily, but to reconstruct what can be asserted and with what degree of confidence.

## What is usually lost

Context, sources, and hypotheses scatter before a usable understanding can form.

## With Koali

Kristal preserves multiple possible identities, their evidence, and family relationships without turning a hypothesis into a fact.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`people → documents → dates → relationships → contradictions → hypotheses → sourced family tree`

find · understand · collaborate · verify · remember
