# SCN-106 — Share the Breakthrough Without Turning “Promising” into “Proven”

> **Universal pattern —** Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-22` Explain publicly without severing sources

## Situation

Researchers, journalists, and citizens can navigate from the summary down to data, limitations, replications, and controversies. Public media can be simple without severing links to complexity.

## What is usually lost

The headline outruns the evidence when limits, confidence, replication, and controversy are cut from the discovery.

## With Koali

Koali creates audience-appropriate explanations from the same governed evidence, letting citizens, journalists, and researchers go deeper without simplification turning into hype.

## Loop

`result → sources → limits → narrative → media → questions → updates`

## Usage palette

understand · teach-share · disseminate · remember

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●○○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **result → … → updates** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

science · health · climate

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.**
