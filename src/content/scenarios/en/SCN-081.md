# SCN-081 — Three Sick Diners Put the Entire Menu Under Suspicion

> **Universal pattern —** Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-16` Turn an incident into coordinated response

## Situation

The team must quickly identify batches, ingredients, suppliers, preparations, and potentially affected people without turning an initial hypothesis into a certain accusation.

## What is usually lost

An early suspicion becomes an accusation when dishes, batches, suppliers, withdrawals, and evidence cannot be traced separately.

## With Koali

Koali follows meals through supplies, lot numbers, and exposed diners while withdrawals and messages proceed, containing risk without converting a hypothesis into an accusation.

## Loop

`reports → batches → recipes → suppliers → hypotheses → precautionary actions → confirmation`

## Usage palette

respond · understand · organize · act · verify

- **Kristal ●●●●●** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **reports → … → confirmation** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.
