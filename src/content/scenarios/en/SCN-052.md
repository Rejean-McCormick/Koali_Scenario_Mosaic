# SCN-052 — After the Legitimacy Crisis, Rebuild a Mandate People Trust

> **Universal pattern —** Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-12` Connect decision, mandate, and consequences

## Situation

After a legitimacy crisis, institutions, communities, the diaspora, and civic groups must rebuild a credible constitutional path without turning the tool into a mechanism for seizing power. The problem is traceability of mandates, proposals, objections, and transition commitments.

## What is usually lost

Representation claims become power claims when mandates, rules, objections, and expiry dates are opaque.

## With Koali

Koali exposes mandates, authority, objections, and expiry dates, helping a constitutional process earn trust without letting the platform turn into a silent power grab.

## Loop

`legitimacy crisis → mandates → proposals → deliberation → legitimate decision → responsibilities → transition`

## Usage palette

understand · deliberate · choose · organize · verify

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●○○○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **legitimacy crisis → … → transition** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to
