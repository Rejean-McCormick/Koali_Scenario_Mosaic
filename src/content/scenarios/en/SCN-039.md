# SCN-039 — Let the Audience Program the Festival—Live

> **Universal pattern —** Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-07` Co-create without losing the why

## Situation

Audience members can propose artists, explain their choices, create pathways, and support unexpected combinations. Organizers retain budget, safety, and diversity constraints while giving the collective real influence.

## What is usually lost

Audience creativity looks arbitrary when constraints and reasons disappear from the final program.

## With Koali

Koali lets ideas meet real constraints in public and records the trade-offs, making the final program both surprising and explainable.

## Loop

`proposals → affinities → constraints → combinations → votes → program → feedback`

## Usage palette

collaborate · create · choose · organize · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●○○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●●** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **proposals → … → feedback** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.**
