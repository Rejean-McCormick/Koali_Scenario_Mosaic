# SCN-089 — The CEO Video Looks Real—even to the CEO's Team

> **Universal pattern —** Prevent teams from acting on different or outdated versions of reality.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-17` Maintain a changing shared situation

## Situation

The reputation crisis also becomes a provenance crisis. The organization must verify sources, respond publicly, and avoid amplifying the deceptive content itself.

## What is usually lost

Verification, response, and publication drift apart, amplifying the fake while weakening the evidence against it.

## With Koali

Koali anchors every response version to verification evidence and distribution choices, helping the organization disprove the fraud without weakening—or spreading—the case against it.

## Loop

`content → provenance → verification → stakeholders → response → follow-up → lesson`

## Usage palette

respond · understand · collaborate · choose · act · disseminate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●●** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●●●○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●○○** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **content → … → lesson** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to
