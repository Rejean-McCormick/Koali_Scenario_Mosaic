# SCN-089 — A fake video of the CEO is so convincing that even employees hesitate to believe it is fake

> **Universal pattern —** Prevent teams from acting on different or outdated versions of reality.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-17` Maintain a changing shared situation

## Situation

The reputation crisis also becomes a provenance crisis. The organization must verify sources, respond publicly, and avoid amplifying the deceptive content itself.

## What is usually lost

Reality changes faster than shared context, so teams risk acting on different or outdated information.

## With Koali

Kristal connects authenticity evidence and communication versions; Orgo coordinates validation and distribution; the reasoning behind the response remains auditable.

The Koali point is continuity: knowledge, people, decisions, work, and memory remain connected to the same context instead of being recreated in separate tools.

## Loop

`content → provenance → verification → stakeholders → response → follow-up → lesson`

respond · understand · collaborate · choose · act · disseminate
