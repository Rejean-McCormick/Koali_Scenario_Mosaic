# SCN-107 — Make the Unpopular Decision Impossible to Misrepresent

> **Universal pattern —** Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.

> **Pattern family —** `PAT-22` Explain publicly without severing sources

## Situation

Instead of a defensive press release, residents can see objectives, rejected options, costs, arguments, and decisions. Disagreements remain visible without making the content unreadable.

## What is usually lost

A simplified announcement becomes propaganda when rejected options, sources, and trade-offs disappear.

## With Koali

Koali lets residents move from plain language into rejected options, costs, evidence, and trade-offs, so simpler wording never has to sever the reasoning beneath it.

## Loop

`decision → reasons → sources → objections → public narrative → questions → updates`

## Usage palette

understand · teach-share · disseminate · collaborate

## Component roles in this scenario

- **Kristal ●●●●○** — keeps the knowledge, provenance, relationships, and memory needed to understand the situation.
- **Konnaxion ●●●●○** — connects the people who can add, challenge, teach, learn, or interpret the shared context.
- **Orgo ●●○○○** — takes over when a check, decision, request, or intervention must become tracked work.
- **UCKK ●●●●●** — supports learning and multimedia dissemination when the knowledge must reach a broader audience.

## What makes this characteristically Koali

The value is not any one isolated feature. It is preserving the same context across **decision → … → updates** so knowledge, people, choices, work, and memory remain connected.

## Transferable to

municipal government · governance · major projects

The domain changes; the pattern remains: **Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.**
