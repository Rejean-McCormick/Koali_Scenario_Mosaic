# Product Definition

## Mission

Allow someone who does not know Koali to **understand its versatility through exploration**, without reading a long presentation or scanning a list of 120 entries.

## User problem

Koali is general-purpose. A list of uses becomes long very quickly; one example becomes too specific; a complete taxonomy demands too much attention.

The Mosaic answers with a discovery experience:

> **I see many possibilities → one cell catches my attention → I hover → I understand a concrete case → I can open the full scenario.**

## Objectives

1. Make the variety felt before explaining it.
2. Enable exploration with no prerequisite learning.
3. Show enough information on hover to decide whether a scenario deserves a click.
4. Give every scenario a stable URL.
5. Keep the site extremely fast.
6. Keep content maintainable in Markdown.
7. Allow future scenarios to be added without automatically redistributing the existing 120.

## MVP non-goals

- explain the full kOA architecture on the mosaic page;
- provide a scientific similarity graph;
- automatically optimize placement;
- personalize the layout per user;
- build a collaborative content-editing tool;
- load a 3D engine;
- turn the page into a complex search product.

## Audience

Curious members of the public, partners, organizations, researchers, developers, and visitors arriving from a Koali presentation.

The system must work even if users do not know the terms Kristal, Konnaxion, and Orgo; these appear as secondary indicators and can be explained progressively.
