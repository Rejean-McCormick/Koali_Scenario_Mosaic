# Preview Editorial Standard

## Purpose

The preview is not the full scenario page. It should provide just enough material to understand the case.

## Summary structure

Recommended formula:

> **Situation.** What is happening. **Koali.** What the system connects. **Outcome.** What becomes possible or better preserved.

## Length

35–70 words, with an approximate maximum of 420 characters for the main preview summary.

## Tone

- concrete;
- no unnecessary jargon;
- avoid “revolutionizes,” “transforms everything,” “infinite intelligence”;
- do not promise an unvalidated capability;
- prefer verbs such as connect, retrieve, coordinate, document, decide, transmit.

## Title

A human situation:

Good: **A team is trying to understand why a machine keeps breaking down.**

Weaker: **Intelligent industrial incident management.**

## Image

Describe a recognizable situation rather than repeating the same abstract metaphor 120 times.
