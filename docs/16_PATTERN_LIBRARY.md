# Library of 24 recurring patterns

This layer is the main structural optimization of v2. It prevents a jump from eight broad usage categories directly to 120 very specific scenarios.

> Pattern families are **editorial abstractions** derived from scenarios and the Koali knowledge-to-action loop. They are not 24 software modules.

## PAT-01 — Investigate a signal

Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.

## PAT-02 — Reconstruct context and provenance

Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

## PAT-03 — Find the right expertise

Connect a question, need, or project with the relevant people and knowledge.

## PAT-04 — Turn practice into transferable knowledge

Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

## PAT-05 — Connect learning, practice, and evidence

Carry learning through activity, evidence, and demonstrated situated capability.

## PAT-06 — Teach across language or connectivity constraints

Preserve meaning and learning continuity when language, network access, or location changes.

## PAT-07 — Co-create without losing the why

Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.

## PAT-08 — Assemble a team around a problem

Identify the required capabilities and form the right collective for a goal.

## PAT-09 — Move an idea to a prototype

Connect exploration, design, testing, decisions, and the next operational step.

## PAT-10 — Compare and prioritize explicitly

Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.

## PAT-11 — Deliberate across perspectives

Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.

## PAT-12 — Connect decision, mandate, and consequences

Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.

## PAT-13 — Deliver work with its context

Give the responsible person the task, the why, the history, the constraints, and the expected evidence of completion.

## PAT-14 — Coordinate roles and dependencies

Maintain a shared work state across people, teams, resources, and constraints.

## PAT-15 — Carry a plan through execution

Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.

## PAT-16 — Turn an incident into coordinated response

Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

## PAT-17 — Maintain a changing shared situation

Prevent teams from acting on different or outdated versions of reality.

## PAT-18 — Keep working in field/offline mode

Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.

## PAT-19 — Turn action into a lesson

Connect results, decisions, and causes into usable lessons learned.

## PAT-20 — See patterns over time

Compare cases, seasons, incidents, or measures to detect recurrence and change.

## PAT-21 — Preserve capability beyond individuals

Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.

## PAT-22 — Explain publicly without severing sources

Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.

## PAT-23 — Turn knowledge into media or learning

Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.

## PAT-24 — Keep media aligned with knowledge

Connect published content, versions, and sources so it can be corrected, superseded, or revised.

## How to use this layer

Each scenario keeps a specific situation but also receives a `pattern_family`. In a website or deck, a pattern page should show examples from several sectors side by side so transferability is immediately visible.
