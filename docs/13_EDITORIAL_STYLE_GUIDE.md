# Editorial style guide

## Lead with the human situation
Bad: “Orgo routes tasks with Kristal context.”
Good: “A technician receives a repair task with the history, evidence, constraints, and reason for the intervention attached.”

## Then reveal the architecture
Once the reader recognizes the problem, explain that Kristal preserves knowledge, Konnaxion brings in people, Orgo organizes action, and UCKK supports dissemination.

## Prefer verbs to nouns
Use: find, understand, learn, collaborate, choose, organize, respond, remember, disseminate.
Avoid dense product-language lists.

## Keep the transferable pattern visible
Every scenario should make clear that the sector is an example, not the product boundary.

## Avoid universal-product language
Do not say “Koali replaces X” unless that is explicitly intended and evidenced. Prefer “Koali connects the context around X,” “Koali can bridge X to Y,” or “Koali supports the workflow around X.”

## Evidence-sensitive language
Use **can**, **could**, **supports**, **is designed to**, and **in this scenario** according to the scenario’s evidence status. Reserve **does**, **is deployed**, and **has demonstrated** for validated claims.
