# Scenario quality rubric

Score each proposed scenario before adding it to the Mosaic.

## 1. Recognizable human problem
Can the reader understand the situation before learning Koali terminology?

## 2. Continuity value
Does the scenario show Koali preserving context across at least two meaningful steps?

## 3. Transferability
Can the same pattern plausibly apply in at least three other domains?

## 4. Component clarity
Are Kristal, Konnaxion, Orgo, and UCKK used only where they genuinely add explanatory value?

## 5. Non-replacement discipline
Does the scenario avoid pretending Koali replaces specialist, certified, or authoritative systems where it does not?

## 6. Evidence discipline
Does wording match `derivation` and `runtime_evidence`?

## 7. Distinctiveness
Does the card show something more interesting than “store a document,” “send a message,” or “create a task” in isolation?

## Acceptance
A strong scenario should pass all seven criteria. Weak scenarios should be merged, rewritten around a stronger pattern, or removed.
